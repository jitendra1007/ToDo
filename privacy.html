Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

Your privacy is fundamentally important to us. This Privacy Policy describes how the Cloud To-Do Checklist application handles data when you use our service.

1. Data Collection & Storage

We do not collect, monitor, track, or store any of your personal data, task details, or credentials on any external third-party servers. Your data stays entirely in your own control at all times through two methods:

  • Local Storage: Your tasks are temporarily saved directly inside your local web browser's cache (LocalStorage).
  • Google Drive Sandbox: When you connect your Google Account, your synchronized backup file (todo_backup.json) is saved inside an isolated, hidden folder application space (appDataFolder) on your own personal Google Drive cloud server.

2. Google Drive Permissions

Our application requests access strictly to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata authentication scope. This restricted permission means our app can only read and write files it created itself. We have absolutely no ability to access, view, or modify any other personal documents, files, or folders in your Google Drive account.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Because we do not collect any data, we cannot and do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third-party companies, advertisers, or individuals.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy framework, you can contact the developer directly through the project's source repository platform profile.
